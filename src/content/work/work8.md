Designed for a summer 2026 launch in Antwerp, the brief required crafting a complete visual identity and brand board from scratch, followed by designing a mobile app prototype that elevates existing wireframes into a seamless, engaging subscription management and cooking experience."
---

I developed a complete brand identity and mobile experience designed to make culinary discovery feel warm, authentic, and accessible. I crafted a custom visual language—including a travel-inspired logo mark, distinct color palettes for each box category, and approachable typography—that reflects the brand's core values. In Figma, I translated these brand foundations into a fully prototyped mobile app, focusing on seamless UI layout, intuitive subscription management, and engaging recipe exploration.
