Visual Design project

Miles & Meals is a visual identity and editorial concept exploring how food, travel, and everyday rituals can be translated into a warm, modern design language. The project focuses on composition, typography, and tone to build a lifestyle brand that feels personal, atmospheric, and easy to navigate.

The work highlights thoughtful layouts, storytelling-driven visuals, and a cohesive visual system designed to feel welcoming and premium without becoming overly polished or sterile.
