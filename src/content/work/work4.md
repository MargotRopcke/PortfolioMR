---

To celebrate NMBS’s 100th anniversary, I focused on train travel as an affordable, spontaneous solution to modern travel fatigue. My 30-second animation highlights the rich culture, activities, and landscapes hidden right in our own backyard, framing NMBS as the effortless link to a domestic getaway. Visualized through a postcard-collecting theme—where each city displays its own distinct visual personality—the piece captures how quickly and simply train travel brings us to our next destination. This project was selected by my university as one of the 12 official entries to represent the school in the national NMBS competition.
