Working in a three-person team (Femke, Margot, & Mila) for agency Craftzing, we tackled how parents navigate early childhood (0–6) screen routines without feeling overwhelmed or guilty. Through an extensive FigJam research pipeline, we structured desk research, conducted qualitative parent interviews, and deployed a custom physical Cultural Probe Box to capture unpolished family moments. Synthesizing our findings into user personas, empathy maps, and key design principles, we translated primary fieldwork insights into a personalized, age-aware digital media framework designed to help parents balance daily convenience, child development, and family peace of mind.
