I designed and developed Conscious Couture, a fashion-magazine-inspired web experience created to help fast-fashion shopaholics break free from overconsumption by adopting an off-grid lifestyle. I focused on combining sustainability with creative self-expression—turning a potential culture shock into a fun, empowering journey. Using a bold, editorial visual language created in Figma, custom illustrations, and quirky off-grid tips (such as upcycling clothes with a pair of scissors or training homing pigeons for delivery), I built a responsive, semantic HTML/CSS site using BEM notation and CSS Grid/Flexbox, published live at margotropcke.be/integration1.
