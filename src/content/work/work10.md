Departing from traditional city marketing, the brief required designing an authentic, non-promotional concept centered on Antwerp’s core themes (Style, Flavors, 24/7 Pulse, Historic Heart with a Modern Beat). The solution required combining at least two key components: an interactive digital application, a physical urban activation, or a social media strategy."

I co-designed The Portal for Visit Antwerp, an interactive physical kiosk and mobile application for young urban travelers. I developed UI layouts, component libraries, and interactive wireframes in Figma to connect an ML5 gesture-driven physical booth with a mobile PWA map interface—delivering personalized local food and fashion routes alongside custom souvenir cards.
