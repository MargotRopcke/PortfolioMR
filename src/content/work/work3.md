I created a cohesive p5.js visual system built around a signature crimson color palette (#7b0323 and its tonal variations) to link all four scenes together. To express the duality of the prompt, I paired Chaos and Order as structural opposites—Chaos breaking a rigid grid with unpredictable random walks and floating geometry, while Order stabilizes it with structured matrix highlights and controlled rotations. Similarly, Dark and Light form a balance through color inversions and movement, transitioning from a calm, phase-shifting celestial night to an expanding, high-fps solar wave animation.
