I designed and animated a commercial motion graphics video for Modica, a healthy cocktail and mocktail mixer on Kickstarter. The promo addresses home-bartending hassle through lively 2D motion design—featuring liquid transitions, ingredient breakdowns (superfoods, B-vitamins, electrolytes), and morphing bottle layouts to position Modica as the ultimate effortless mixology solution.
