Motion Design project

Modica is a conceptual motion identity project exploring how a brand can express clarity, emotion, and rhythm through motion. The goal was to design a short visual story that feels premium, modern, and memorable while remaining simple and intentional.

The animation focuses on typography, pacing, and transitions to create a high-end atmosphere. It combines minimal forms with fluid movement, building a brand presence that feels both polished and expressive.
