For the Project Hyperion generation starship challenge, my team designed MARA (Material Access & Repair Assistant)—an integrated wall-dispenser and digital touchscreen system that helps young adults transition to independent living within extreme spatial constraints. Built on three core pillars (Fostering Autonomy, Sustainable Design, and Expressing Identity), MARA connects individual quarters to a central supply hub. When everyday household issues arise, MARA dispenses shared tools, guides residents through step-by-step troubleshooting, detects stress via fingerprint biosensors to provide calming sensory feedback, and offers privacy-controlled peer tips to turn everyday fixes into confidence-building milestones.
